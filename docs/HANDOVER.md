# N.I.P.P.E.R.S. website — guide for the committee

## Signing in
Go to **nippers.org.uk/login** and use the admin email and password you were given. Change the password straight away under **Settings** (bottom of the admin menu). To give a colleague admin access: **Admin → Parents**, find them (they need to have created a parent account first) and change their role to *Admin*.

## Day to day
- **Dashboard** — pending requests, this week's numbers, unread messages, next sessions.
- **Bookings** — filter by status/date, tick several and **Confirm**/**Decline** in one go, or click a booking to add a note (the note is emailed when declining). Parents get an email whenever a status changes. **CSV** exports the list for your accounts.
- **Register** — pick a day and press **Print**. Shows every child booked, their allergies/medical notes, emergency contacts, parent phone numbers and a camera icon if photo consent has been given. Tick boxes for signing in/out.
- **Children / Parents** — everything families have entered. Disable an account to block sign-in without deleting anything.
- **Messages** — contact-form enquiries (also emailed to the office).

## Set-up (do these once a year)
- **Sessions & prices** — change prices, times, capacity (max children per session per day), add or hide sessions.
- **Calendar** — enter the East Sussex school holiday dates each year. Weekdays inside a holiday get Holiday Club sessions; all other weekdays get After School Club. Add bank holidays, Christmas week and INSET days as **Closures**. The minimum-notice setting stops last-minute online bookings.
- **Settings** — phone, email, address, payment instructions and (optionally) bank details, which only signed-in parents with confirmed bookings can see. Untick **Online bookings open** to pause requests.

## Website content
- **Testimonials** — keep them GDPR-safe: "Parent of a Year 2 child", not full names, unless you have written permission.
- **Policies** — each policy is a title, one-line summary and full text (blank line between paragraphs).
- **Announcements** — a banner at the top of every page between two dates, e.g. "Summer bookings now open".
- **Gallery** — upload photos straight from a phone; they're resized automatically. Only upload children whose parents have given photo consent (check the register icon).

## What parents see
Create account → add each child once → tap days on the calendar → choose sessions → send request. They get an email when you confirm, with your payment instructions. They can cancel upcoming sessions themselves (your cancellation policy still applies) and update children's details at any time — the register always prints the latest information.
